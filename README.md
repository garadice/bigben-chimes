# Big Ben Chimes 🔔

A tiny ad-free app for one specific 6-year-old: a big photo of Big Ben and
giant buttons that make it really chime. The sounds are genuine recordings of
Big Ben from 1 January 1945 (public domain), the photo is openly licensed.

- **🔔 ZVONY** — plays the full quarter chime sequence, then strikes the hour
  the way Big Ben really does (at 15:00 you hear 3 bongs, at midnight 12).
- **🛎 BONGY** — just the hour bongs.
- **⏰ KAŽDÚ ŠTVRŤ** — auto-chime every quarter hour while the app is open:
  melody at :15/:30/:45, full sequence + hour bongs on the hour.
- 🔊 slider + mute in the corner, big live clock up top.

## Install on Android (Chrome)

1. Open the site URL in Chrome.
2. Menu (⋮) → **Pridať na domovskú obrazovku / Add to Home screen**.
3. Launch it from the home screen — it runs full-screen and works offline
   after the first load (all sounds and the photo are cached on the device).

First tap unlocks audio (Android rule) — after that every button just works.
